inii / CBD Cosmetics /

inii cosmetics is a brand based in Haina, Canada. It sells products infused with CBD oil extracted from indigenous cannabis with the package made from its stem, the by-product of cannabis. The package is designed with a moon cut out on the wrap and opens to showcase a sun on the carton to imply our product gives your skin a night and day difference. The logo is designed with cursive writing to symbolize the free and casual characteristics of cannabis products.
This project is the winner of
2021 GDUSA American Packaged Design Award.
