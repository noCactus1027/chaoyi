Kali / Period Normalization /

Although most women bleed or experience bleeding every month, this “thing” that proves a woman is healthy enough for procreation somehow gets interpreted into a shameful phenomenon. Women tend to hide tampons and the fact that they’re on their period to avoid awkward moments. This results in tampon packages designed mainly for hygienic and discreteness. This set of tampon packages are designed to be left out in a bathroom like a normal roll of toilet paper. Everyone shits, every woman bleeds.
