Me-Time / Sex Positivity /

Me time is a unisex sexual pleasure brand. A bundle of 5 products including vibrator, lubricant, private part wash, condoms, candle and incense are developed for consumers to grasp and extend the euphoria of climax while abolishing the paranoia of privacy invasion. The set is designed to beautifully and confidently present sexual activity while keeping “me time” with me only. 5 products sitting side by side on the desk forms a line of moon faces shining with iridescent sequins from a bird eye perspective. Just like the moon, sexual activities requires some darkness (privacy) to accentuate the sparkling satisfication. Comparing to the hot pink vibrators commonly seen on the market that shouts lewdness, the color palette and materials are chosen to articulate the intimate but spectacular aura of sex.
