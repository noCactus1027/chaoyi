Tampons & Pads / Menstrual Products /

This rectangular box appears to be simple and basic. It is actually capable of dividing and dispensing two different absorbencies of tampons as desired. The outer box contains two separate tear-strip openings for two absorbencies, one located on the bottom of the front panel while the other is on the top of the back panel. A separated paper structure is designed to fit into the outer box to gravity dispense desired flow while cease dispensing the other. Simply flip the package upside down, front side back to dispense the other absorbency of tampons
