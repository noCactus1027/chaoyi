Herbal Tea / Package Design /

This Taiwanese brand is aiming to give their brand a whole new look to attract young people so they can try out their healthy and delicious Chinese herbal tea and fall in love with it.
I designed five sets of labels and one sticker. Each contains the most used herb of that tea in their plant form.
